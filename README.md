# WATEL — Waste to Electrical Energy

### Transforming Waste into a Valuable Energy Resource

Welcome to WATEL, a web-based platform designed to promote responsible waste management and support the conversion of waste into electrical energy. My goal is to encourage communities in Cameroon to see waste not simply as something to discard, but as a resource with the potential to create value.

WATEL aims to connect waste management, energy recovery, and community participation through an accessible digital platform.

## Project Objectives

WATEL aims to:

- **Promote Responsible Waste Management:** Encourage users to dispose of waste responsibly and contribute to cleaner communities.
- **Monitor Energy Consumption:** Allow users to view information about their electricity consumption.
- **Track Waste Contributions:** Enable users to monitor the quantity of waste they contribute over time.
- **Display Electricity Bill Information:** Give users access to their electricity bill details and applicable reductions, where available.
- **Encourage Community Participation:** Explore ways to reward eligible users for contributing waste to the energy recovery process.
- **Provide Educational Resources:** Share informative videos and resources that explain environmental protection, waste management, recycling, and waste-to-energy technologies.
- **Support Communication:** Provide a way for users to contact the WATEL team for assistance, questions, or feedback.

## Key Website Features

### 1. User Authentication
Users can sign in to an existing account or create a new account to access the platform's features.

### 2. Energy Dashboard
Users can view their available energy information and monitor their electricity consumption.

### 3. Waste Contribution Tracker
Users can check the quantity of waste they have contributed and review their monthly contribution history.

### 4. Electricity Bill Management
Users can view their electricity bill information and check any applicable reductions or reward credits supported by the platform.

### 5. Customer Support
Users can contact the WATEL team to report issues, ask questions, and receive assistance.

### 6. Environmental Education
Users can access educational videos and other learning materials that explain the importance of responsible waste management and the potential of waste-to-energy solutions.

## Our Vision

Our vision is to contribute to a cleaner and more sustainable Cameroon by encouraging responsible waste management and exploring innovative ways to recover energy from waste.

We believe that combining technology, environmental awareness, and community participation can help create a future in which waste is managed more responsibly and its potential value is not overlooked.

## Project Status

WATEL is a developing project. Some features, including live electricity monitoring, verified waste-to-energy production data, and electricity bill rewards, may require further development and partnerships with relevant waste management and electricity providers.

The website's interface and prototype features are intended to demonstrate how such a system could work in practice.

## Project Mission

**Turning waste into opportunity, supporting cleaner communities, and exploring a smarter energy future for Cameroon.**
