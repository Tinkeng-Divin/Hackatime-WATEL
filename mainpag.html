    Frequently Asked Questions

    Find answers to common questions about WATEL.

    What is WATEL?

    WATEL stands for Waste to Electrical Energy. It is a platform designed to support the transformation of waste into useful electrical energy.

    How can I contribute waste?

    You can contribute eligible waste through the WATEL waste collection system.

    How can I check my energy balance?

    Visit the Statistics section to view your energy information.

    How can I view my electricity bills?

    Click Electric Bills on your dashboard to access your payment information.

    How can I contact WATEL?

    You can contact the WATEL support team or submit your feedback through the Feedback section.
